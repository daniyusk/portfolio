## Platform

web

## Users

- Primary audience for the current stage: prospective freelance clients evaluating whether Daniel can build the kind of software or interactive experience they need.
- Secondary audience: recruiters, studios, development teams, and other developers evaluating Daniel's work and technical profile.
- Visitors commonly arrive with limited time and need to understand what Daniel produces without reading a long résumé.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio exists to make Daniel's real work, technical range, and developer identity easy to understand. Success means a visitor can quickly inspect representative projects, recognize the kind of systems Daniel builds, and reach a clear path to contact him about freelance work or another professional opportunity.

## Positioning

Daniel combines systems-oriented software development, reactive interfaces, game-system work, and algorithmic reasoning. The portfolio should differentiate him through inspectable project evidence and documented mathematical achievements rather than broad, unsupported claims.

## Operating Context

- Visitors scan the portfolio on desktop or mobile, inspect featured projects and their media, read the About section for context and credibility, and continue to contact options.
- The About section must complement the project gallery instead of repeating it or becoming a long résumé.
- The current public identity is `Daniyusk`; the visible full-name presentation is `Daniel Senzaki`. Official academic award lists use `Daniel Yutaka Senzaki`.

- Do not claim scalability, production reliability, performance results, client outcomes, or other measurable impact without concrete supporting evidence.

## Brand Commitments

## Product Principles

1. Show the work before making the claim.
2. Make the produced work understandable within a quick scan.
3. Use concise, verifiable proof instead of résumé-length explanation.
4. Serve freelance prospects now without excluding recruiters or development teams.
5. Preserve a clear route from project evidence to contact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- The portfolio must remain understandable and usable on mobile devices and for visitors with low vision.
- Important meaning must not depend on color, hover, or continuous animation alone.
- Content and layout must remain legible and coherent at 200% browser zoom and with reduced motion enabled.
- Text and interactive elements should meet WCAG AA contrast expectations.
